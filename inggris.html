TUGAS IZZAM WAHYU FADHILA

How an Old Circuit Boards Is Transformed into a Well Clock

Paragraph 1 - General Statement
Electronic waste is an increasing environmental issue caused by the rapid pace of technological advancement. Obsolete electronic components, such as discarded computer circuit boards, are frequently thrown away and contribute to environmental degradation. However, these materials possess complex designs and durable structures that can be repurposed. One creative approach to handling this electronic waste is by transforming an old circuit board into a unique wall clock. This transformation demonstrates how industrial waste can be converted into a functional and artistic home accessory.
​ Paragraph 2 - The Transformation Process
The process begins by collecting an unused computer circuit board and cleaning it thoroughly to remove dust, grease, and other debris. Once the board is clean, technicians or crafters inspect its surface and carefully remove any hazardous or protruding parts that are unnecessary for the new design. At this stage, the original function of the printed circuit board as an internal computer component changes. Instead of processing electronic signals inside a computer, the board is prepared to serve as the main face and structural base of a timepiece.
​Paragraph 3 - The Formation of a New Product
The prepared circuit board is then combined with a clock mechanism and custom number markers. A hole is drilled precisely in the center of the board to install the clock movement, along with the hour, minute, and second hands on the front. Colorful or metallic numbers representing digits one through twelve are securely attached around the perimeter of the board to indicate the hours. Once the battery-powered mechanism is fitted at the back and all components are firmly fixed, the separate parts integrate into a single working clock.
​ ​Paragraph 4 - The Result and Its Impact
As a result, an old computer circuit board is successfully transformed into a wall clock that carries both functional and aesthetic value. The product can be used to tell time while simultaneously serving as a creative, geek-chic room decoration. This transformation reduces the volume of electronic waste and promotes the reuse of durable industrial materials. Therefore, recycling electronic components into creative timepieces is an effective way to support a more sustainable and eco-friendly environment.

TUGAS MELINDA IKA SETYAWATI

The Creation of a Circuit Board Wall Clock from E-Waste

Paragraph 1 - General Statement
The rapid evolution of modern technology has led to a massive accumulation of electronic waste worldwide. Discarded motherboards and circuit boards are typical examples of e-waste that often end up in landfills, posing threats to the environment. Nevertheless, the intricate patterns and metallic traces on these boards offer high visual appeal. A brilliant solution to utilize this waste is by converting old circuit boards into decorative wall clocks. This creative recycling effort turns electronic scrap into a stylish instrument for measuring time.
Paragraph 2 - The Transformation Process
The conversion process starts with the selection of a discarded circuit board featuring prominent electronic tracks, chips, and soldered joints. This board undergoes a cleaning and sanitization procedure to ensure it is safe and free from grime. During this phase, the component is stripped of its original utility within a computer system. Rather than managing data processing, the circuit board is repurposed as a decorative canvas and structural foundation for a new clock.
Paragraph 3 - The Formation of a New Product
Next, a clock movement kit consisting of a motor mechanism and hands is integrated into the circuit board. A central hole is created to mount the spindle, connecting the rear motor to the front indicator hands. Distinctive number labels are arranged and glued onto the surface of the green board to complete the clock face. When the mechanical parts are fully assembled and tested with a power source, the individual pieces merge into a cohesive finished product.
Paragraph 4 - The Result and Its Impact
Consequently, a discarded circuit board is turned into an innovative wall clock that provides practical utility and decorative charm. It functions reliably as a timekeeping device while adding a modern, technological aesthetic to interior spaces. Furthermore, this method helps minimize electronic waste and encourages sustainable resource management. Upcycling electronic parts into functional art represents a meaningful contribution toward environmental preservation.

TUGAS OKTAVIANI CAESARA HANDAYANI

Upcycling Discarded Circuit Boards into Fuctional Clocks

Paragraph 1 - General Statement
Electronic equipment has a relatively short lifecycle, resulting in vast quantities of electronic waste discarded each year. Printed circuit boards are among the most common items found in this waste stream, traditionally viewed as useless junk. However, their intricate microchips and vibrant green layouts hold significant creative potential. By upcycling these old circuit boards into functional wall clocks, people can give electronic remnants a second life. This process highlights how creativity can merge technology with interior design.
Paragraph 2 - The Transformation Process
The manufacturing workflow begins by gathering retired circuit boards and cleaning their surfaces to eliminate impurities. Workers inspect the boards to identify the best layout angles for a clock face. At this point, the primary purpose of the circuit board undergoes a total shift. Instead of functioning as part of a hidden computer hardware system, it is modified to become a visible, artistic centerpiece for time tracking.
Paragraph 3 - The Formation of a New Product
The transformation is finalized by attaching a clock mechanism to the center of the board and mounting hands on the front side. Clear, stylized numbers are positioned around the board's surface to guide the eye and show the hours accurately. Once the adhesive sets and the battery mechanism is secured in place, all the disparate elements combine into a single, operational clock product.
Paragraph 4 - The Result and Its Impact
Ultimately, the old electronic component becomes a striking wall clock that delivers both utility and visual appeal. It serves as a practical tool for daily scheduling and a conversation starter due to its unique technological look. This upcycling practice successfully cuts down electronic waste and inspires alternative ways to reuse manufactured materials. Thus, turning electronic hardware into household items is a great step toward environmental sustainability.
